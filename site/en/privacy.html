Your data

Privacy policy

In short: no account required, no ads, no analytics. Your favourites and settings stay on your device.

What the app stores

MISSA TV 360 PLUS requires no account and contains no ads, no analytics and no trackers. We receive no personal data from the app.

Your favourites, watch history, settings, parental PIN and downloads are stored only on your device. If Android backup is enabled on your device, your settings and favourites may be backed up by Android to your Google account; sign-in credentials for third-party services are excluded.

If the app crashes, an error report is written on the device. It is never sent automatically.

Services the app connects to

To work, the app connects directly from your device to third-party services. As with any Internet connection, these services see your IP address. Each applies its own privacy policy:

  • Radio: station directories (Radio Browser, SomaFM, Laut.fm, the Icecast directory) and the streams of the stations you listen to.
  • Podcasts: the Apple Podcasts directory, to find shows, and the RSS feeds of the shows you open.
  • Music and Kids: YouTube, to search and play tracks and videos.
  • Logos and images: the stations' websites, Wikimedia and Google's icon service.
  • Updates: GitHub, to check for and download new versions.
  • Games: GitHub, to read the catalogue and download games and their emulators. Once installed, games run offline and their saves stay on the device.
  • Extensions: the extensions you install connect to their own websites, under their own rules. We have no control over them.

Optional accounts

If you wish, you can connect a tracking or subtitle account (for example AniList, MyAnimeList, Kitsu, Simkl, OpenSubtitles or SubDL). Sign-in credentials stay on your device and are only used to talk to the chosen service. You can sign out at any time in the settings.

Voice search

Voice search uses your device's speech recognition service (often provided by Google), only when you press the microphone.

This website

The main address of this website, missatv.pages.dev, is hosted by Cloudflare Pages, which processes visitors' IP addresses to deliver and protect the service. A backup copy is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for the security of the service (see GitHub's privacy statement). The website uses no cookies and no analytics. To show the latest version, your browser queries GitHub's public API.

Your rights

As we keep no data about you, there is nothing to access or delete on our side. To erase the app's data, clear its storage in Android settings or uninstall it. For any question, write to us from the Contact page.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with its update date.
