Installation

Installed in a few minutes

The app installs straight from this website, on any Android device.

On a phone or tablet

  1. Tap Download the APK on your device.
  2. Open the downloaded file.
  3. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps, then go back.
  4. Tap Install. You're done.

On Android TV, a TV box or Fire TV

  1. Install the free Downloader app from your TV's store.
  2. In Downloader, type the address of this page:
    missatv.pages.dev/en
  3. Select Download the APK with the remote.
  4. Allow Downloader to install unknown apps, then Install.

Updates install over the previous version: your favourites and settings are kept.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

How much does the app cost?

You can try it free for 24 hours. After that, the subscription costs 1,000 FCFA a month or 10,000 FCFA a year, for 2 devices, payable by Mobile Money or card via KPay. The source code remains public, under the GPL-3.0 licence.

Where do the movies and series come from?

MISSA TV 360 PLUS does not host or provide any movie, series or channel, nor any source or extension. The video universes are filled by the extensions you choose to add, under your sole responsibility. Free radio and podcasts work right away, without any extension.

Why isn't the app on the Play Store?

It is distributed directly from GitHub. The downloaded file is built automatically from the public code, and you can check its SHA-256 fingerprint on the release page.

Android says "unknown app". Is that normal?

Yes: Android shows it for any app installed outside the Play Store. Only download it from this page or from the official GitHub page.

Which devices are supported?

Phones, tablets, Android TV, Android TV boxes and Fire TV running Android 6 or later.
