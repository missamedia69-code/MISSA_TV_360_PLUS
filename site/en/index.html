Frequently asked questions

How much does the app cost?

Try it free for 24 hours. Then the subscription costs 1,000 FCFA per month (1 to 9 months) or 10,000 FCFA for a year, for 2 devices, payable by Mobile Money or card via KPay.

How do I activate the app?

When the trial ends, the app shows a code and, on TV, a QR code. Scan it or open missatv.pages.dev/activer, pick a duration and pay: the app unlocks by itself within seconds.

Where do movies and series come from?

MISSA TV 360 PLUS hosts and provides no movies, series or channels, and no sources or extensions. Video universes fill up with the extensions you choose to add, at your own responsibility. Radio and podcasts work right away.

Android says “unknown app”, is that normal?

Yes: Android shows it for any app installed outside the Play Store. Only download it from this site or the official GitHub page.

Which devices are supported?

Phones, tablets, Android TV, Android boxes and Fire TV running Android 6 or later.
